Chosen: O3 Night Orbit

Chosen mark · used in the top bar and on sign-in

O3Night Orbitchosen

Made for the Night Board's rule that white is the only interface colour. The mark is all white, and a white moon circles it. Every few seconds a short light in the three OS colours sweeps once around the orbit. Colour appears only in motion, so the logo sits quietly in the top bar.

Alternatives · not used

O1Orbitalternative

The Orbit mark, refined. The legs of the A rise to the apex node, then the orbit draws on in the three OS colours. A moon circles it and passes behind the A on the far side, so the mark has real depth. The apex node breathes slowly, like the hub of the cluster.

O2Systemalternative

The logo becomes the product. Three moons in the OS colours (Windows, macOS, Linux) circle the solid inner orbit, like hosts around the hub. A small hollow moon runs faster on a dashed outer orbit, the same mark the Orbit design used for test VMs. The whole system sways a little. At small sizes it falls back to the plain O1 mark.
